# Demo flow

The FT (Fedor Testing) user journey, in the order it is demoed. Start logged out at `index.html`. Any email and any non-empty password are accepted.

## Part 1: Sign in

1. Land on the homepage logged out. Header shows **Subscribe** and **Sign In**.
2. Click **Sign In**. Enter an email address and click **Next**.
3. Enter a password and click **Sign in**.
4. On the "Set up a passkey" screen, click **Not now**.
5. Return to the homepage logged in. Header shows **My Account**, and the nav gains FT Digital Edition, Portfolio and myFT.
6. Scroll the full homepage: Top Stories, Spotlight, News, Opinion, Companies, Europe News, Podcasts, Life & Arts, Video, Markets News, Technology, Events, footer.

## Part 2: Navigate and save

7. Click the **burger menu** in the top left. The drawer opens with Edition and Top sections.
8. Click the chevron on **World** to expand it.
9. Click **Global Economy**. The World › Global Economy stream page opens with the "Added" topic pill.
10. Click **Save** on the second article (the FirstFT one). The button turns red and reads Saved, and the myFT popover appears.
11. Click the button again. It unsaves and returns to its initial state.

## Part 3: Read and share

12. Click the second article headline to open the FirstFT article.
13. Scroll through the article: newsletter box, bullet summary, vote-share chart, Five more top stories, Russia's new drive to crush Ukraine, We're also reading, Chart of the day, Take a break from the news.
14. Continue to the bottom: Latest on World grid, then the Comments section.
15. Go back to the top and click the **X** button on the share rail.

## Part 4: Search

16. Click the **search icon** in the masthead. The search overlay opens.
17. Type "Dalio". Suggestions appear: Top results, Related Pages, Securities.
18. Press **Search**. The results page opens for "Dalio".
19. Click the first result, "China's tribute system and the new world order". The Ray Dalio opinion article opens.

## Reset

20. **My Account**, then **Sign out**, returns the site to the logged-out state so the journey can be repeated.

## Pages touched, in order

`index.html` → `login.html` → `passkey.html` → `index.html` → `global-economy.html` → `firstft.html` → `search.html?q=Dalio` → `article-dalio.html` → `account.html`
