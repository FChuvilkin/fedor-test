Bank of England holds rates as inflation stalls above target

Fedor Testing in London · 2 October 2026

The Bank of England left interest rates unchanged on Thursday, pausing a cycle of cuts that began last year, after its committee split almost down the middle over whether price pressures had eased enough.

The Monetary Policy Committee voted 5-4 to hold the bank rate at 4 per cent. Headline inflation has drifted sideways at just under 3 per cent for three months, above the 2 per cent target.

The next decision is due on 18 December. Markets assign roughly a 55 per cent probability to a quarter-point cut at that meeting.
